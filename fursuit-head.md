# Fursuit head fabrication

*Independent project — ongoing*

A wearable fursuit head, modified from a premade base pattern to fit a
personal design and printed/built from scratch using a school-lab Prusa
printer. The project covers pattern adaptation, FDM printing under real
build-volume constraints, foam and fur construction, and (in progress)
ventilation integration.

---

### Goal

Adapt an existing head pattern to a personal design, work within the
constraints of a shared school 3D printer, and build a comfortable,
wearable shell using foam and fabric construction techniques —
with planned ventilation for long-wear comfort.

---

### Materials

- PLA filament, printed on a Prusa FDM printer
- Upholstery foam (interior padding)
- EVA foam (articulated features — ears, accessories)
- Faux fur (outer shell)
- Duct tape (fur and lining pattern-making)
- Rotary tool / Dremel (vent hole cutting)
- Fans (sourced, ventilation integration planned)

**4. Eye blanks, Foam, and Base**

Made the eye blanks to fit the modified head shape. Lined the interior with upholstery foam for padding and comfort. Built the ears and other floppy accessories out of EVA foam.

**7. Faux fur shell**

Applied the faux fur outer shell using the patterns.

**8. Ventilation holes**

Cut vent holes with a rotary tool after gluing on the foam ears.

**9. Finished head**

Added finishing touches such as EVA foam eyelashes, heart nose, and neodymium magnets to express emotions through eyebrows.
